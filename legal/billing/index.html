Billing policy

How Hebra Cloud charges, and what it never does. Hebra Community, the self-hosted edition, is free.

Hebra Community (self-hosted) is free and open source, forever. This policy covers Hebra Cloud (hebra.dev), the hosted service. Prices are in US dollars, before taxes. The machine-readable version of the plans is deploy/billing/plans.toml; the web pricing page is generated from it.

Principles

  • People are seats; agents are not. You pay per person in an organization. Agents (and runners) are unlimited on every plan, within fair rate limits.
  • What costs us money is metered, never surprising. Agent verification uses AI models; storage uses disks. Plans include an allowance; anything above it is billed by use, only up to a spending limit you set (zero by default).
  • Your data is never held hostage. Missing a payment can make private repositories read-only; it never deletes anything without notice, and you can always export.

Agent credits

Agent verification (a check that runs Hebra's verifier agent against your contract) uses AI models. It is measured in credits:

  • 1 credit = $0.01 of model usage at Hebra's price, which is the model provider's list price plus 30% (infrastructure, gateway, safety limits).
  • A typical verification of a small change uses 20–80 credits; a large change can use several hundred. Each run has a hard cap (by default 500 credits) so one run can never consume a month.
  • Included credits renew on the first day of each billing period and do not roll over. Credits above the allowance cost $1 per 100 credits, billed at the end of the period, up to your spending limit.
  • The run log shows the credits each verification used.

Storage

Storage counts the compressed, deduplicated size of every repository an organization owns, measured daily; the month's charge uses the average. Above the allowance: $0.10 per GB per month, within the spending limit.

Cloud runners

Not available yet. When they are, they will be billed per minute with an allowance per plan, and this policy will be updated at least 30 days before any charge applies. Your own runners are always free.

Spending limits

Every organization has a monthly spending limit for usage above its plan (agent credits, storage, runner minutes). It is $0 by default: when the allowance is used up, agent checks report skipped: credit limit reached instead of charging. Owners and billing managers can raise it at any time; Hebra emails them at 75% and 100% of the limit.

Payments and invoices

  • Subscriptions are charged in advance at the start of each period; usage above the allowance is charged at the end of the period.
  • Adding people mid-period is prorated to the day; removing people takes effect at the next period (no partial refunds).
  • Upgrades apply immediately (prorated); downgrades apply at the end of the current period.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. Hebra never sees or stores card numbers.
  • Prices exclude taxes; VAT and sales tax are calculated by Stripe Tax from your billing address and shown on every invoice.

Failed payments

  1. Stripe retries the payment over 14 days and Hebra emails the owners.
  2. After 14 days the organization moves to the Free plan's limits: private repositories above the Free limit become read-only (nothing is deleted), and usage above the allowance stops.
  3. Paying the outstanding invoice restores everything immediately.

Cancellation and data

  • You can cancel any time from the billing settings; the plan stays active until the end of the paid period, then the organization moves to Free.
  • Nothing is deleted when you cancel or downgrade. Repositories that exceed the Free limits stay readable and exportable (hb pull, hb git export).
  • Deleting an organization removes its data within 30 days (backups within 90 days).

Refunds

Payments are not refundable except where the law requires it (for example the statutory withdrawal right of consumers in the EU, where applicable) or when Hebra fails its uptime commitment, in which case service credits apply as described in the plan's terms.

Fair use

Unlimited agents and runners are subject to rate limits that protect the service for everyone (per token and per IP; see the API documentation). Automated abuse (mining, spam, attacks on third parties) ends the service without refund.

Changes to prices

Price increases are announced by email at least 30 days before they apply and never affect a period already paid.

This policy is a product draft. Before charging customers, have it reviewed by a lawyer for each market Hebra Cloud sells in.
