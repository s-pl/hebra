Questions

Do agents count as seats?+

No. You pay for the people in an organization. Agents and runners are unlimited on every plan, within rate limits that keep the service fair for everyone.

What is an agent credit?+

Agent verification runs Hebra's verifier, an AI model, against the contract in your checks. One credit is one cent of model usage at our price: the provider's list price plus 30%. Every run shows the credits it used, and each run stops at a hard cap.

Can I get a surprise bill?+

No. Usage above your plan is charged only up to the spending limit you set, and that limit starts at $0. When the allowance runs out, agent checks are skipped until you raise it or the month renews. We email owners at 75% and 100%.

Is the self-hosted version really free?+

Yes. Hebra Community is Apache 2.0: run it on your own machines, for any number of people, forever. It has everything except the features that need us to operate them (agent verification, the merge queue and the public log witness).

How do checks work without cloud runners?+

You connect your own machines with hb runner register and hb runner start. They take checks from the queue over an outbound connection, so nothing needs to be exposed. Your own runners are free on every plan. Cloud runners are coming; we will announce their price at least 30 days before charging for them.

What happens if I stop paying?+

Nothing is deleted. After 14 days of failed payments the organization moves to the Free plan's limits: private repositories above them become read-only, and you can still export everything.

Discounts?+

Yearly billing is 10 months for 12. Open source projects and students can ask for the Team plan for free.

The full rules, from proration to refunds, are in the billing policy. Prices in US dollars, before taxes.
