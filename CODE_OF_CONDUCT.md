# Code of conduct

Hebra follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and welcoming, assume good faith, criticize ideas rather than people, and accept that maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributors that do not follow these rules.

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through the contact on the [GitHub profile of s-pl](https://github.com/s-pl). Reports are handled confidentially.
